## Profile Naming Decision

The two profiles shipped by `/streamline-approvals` remain:
- **`auto`** — adds `Agent` and `Task` to `~/.claude/settings.json` `permissions.allow`. Sprint Coordinator spawns proceed without per-call prompts.
- **`manual`** — removes `Agent` and `Task` from `permissions.allow`. Each Sprint Coordinator spawn requires a per-call permission prompt from the Conductor.

The plan doc proposed renaming `manual` → `default`. After Tim review (S30 Q3 Option B confirmed), the name `manual` is retained. Rationale: `manual` is more intuitive (`default` invites the question "default of what?"), is consistent with `operatingMode: "manual"` in `.claude/settings.json`, and avoids a user-visible breaking change for anyone who has previously run `/streamline-approvals manual`.
